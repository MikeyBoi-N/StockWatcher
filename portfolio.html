How the plan is built

Written for people and for AI agents; it states what every figure on this page is. Source of truth: js/portfolio.js, with js/engine.js, js/edge.js and js/strategy.js. Rule-based research, not investment advice. No stock pick on this page has a demonstrated edge: the paper record on the Forecaster page has not passed any hypothesis yet. The plan leans on diversification, not on the picks.

Allocation

  • Risk preference: the slider sets the share of the account in stocks, 0–90% in 5% steps; the rest is the core, SPY. The named stops are Conservative 20%, Balanced 40% (the default) and Active 70%. The most stocks and the cap per stock are interpolated between anchors (5% → 3 stocks, 3% each; 20% → 5, 5%; 40% → 8, 8%; 70% → 12, 12%; 90% → 15, 15%), and each sector is held to twice the per-stock cap.
  • Exclusions: tickers you exclude are never picked; the next eligible stock takes the slot. The core can't be excluded. Kept in this browser.
  • Stocks: tracked non-ETF stocks rated Buy zone, then Strong asset (Watch and Avoid are excluded). Each group is ordered by the frozen H2 signal model's expected return relative to the other stocks (unproven), then by Overall, up to the risk preference's number of names.
  • Weights by risk: inverse annualized volatility (the build's GJR-GARCH 21-session forecast, else 30-day realized), so each stock carries similar risk. Water-filled under the per-stock cap, then each sector scaled to at most twice that. Weight the caps free goes back to the core, never to the remaining stocks, so a narrow list means more index, not more concentration.
  • Shares: fractional shares are cut to 0.0001 with a $1 minimum order; with whole shares only, anything that can't buy one share goes to the core, and a core that can't buy one share stays in cash.

Methods (the first that applies wins)

  • Cash-secured put: a Strong stock, options allowed, the at-the-money option liquid (open interest ≥ 500, spread ≤ 6%), implied volatility ≥ 1.3 × the GARCH forecast for the option's life, no earnings within 14 days, and the position's cash covering 100 × a strike at or below the top of the preferred entry zone. The premium is Black-Scholes at the 25-delta put IV.
  • Lump sum: the core and Buy-zone stocks. Buy-zone holdings note the price above which reward/risk drops under 1.5 : 1. Lump sum has beaten spreading purchases out about two times in three historically, because markets tend to rise while cash waits.
  • Limit at preferred entry: a Strong stock within 3% of the top of its preferred entry zone. Fill odds by distance were measured on the replay (12,000 stock-weeks, one-month window): within 3%, 77%; 3–7%, 50%; 7–15%, 21%; beyond 15%, 3%. Waiting and buying at once came out even there, so if the limit hasn't filled by the end of the holding period (at most 30 days) the plan buys at market.
  • DCA schedule: other Strong stocks. Equal weekly slices on weekdays across the holding period (holding days ÷ 7, between 1 and 6 slices), reduced until each slice buys at least the minimum order.
  • Covered call (an overlay): any holding of 100+ shares when options are expensive by the same test: sell one call per 100 shares at the strike at or above the engine's target, for the quoted expiration. It gives up gains above the strike.

Risk figures

  • Single-index model: each holding's variance is split into a market part β²σ²m and a stock-specific part σ² − β²σ²m, the specific parts uncorrelated. Portfolio variance σ²p = (Σ wβ)²σ²m + Σ w²(σ² − β²σ²m), with w the share of the account (cash has no risk). β is the build's one-year beta to SPY, shrunk a third toward 1; missing betas are taken as 1. σm is SPY's forecast volatility.
  • Diversification ratio: the weighted average of the holdings' volatilities ÷ the portfolio's. Above 1 means spreading out removed risk.
  • 90% range: ±1.645 σp √(holding days ÷ 365) × account, a normal approximation; real returns have fatter tails.
  • Expected edge over the market: Σ w × the H2 model's expected 21-session return relative to the other stocks, scaled to the holding period. It is unproven and usually a few hundredths of a percent; treat it as zero until H2 passes. Costs are 0.05% of the money invested, to buy in.

Limitations

  • No correlations beyond the market factor (two chip stocks move together more than this assumes; the sector caps limit that). No taxes, no rebalancing rule, no dividends in the figures. The tracked list leans to large US technology names, so the sector caps often send money back to the core. Option prices other than the at-the-money call are model estimates.
  • For machine use, the verdicts and levels behind this plan are in the JSON API (api/v1/); the Forecaster page's technical reference defines the engine, signals and tests.
