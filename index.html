Four scores answer different questions: Quality (is it a strong business), Trend (is it in a healthy uptrend), Entry (is today's price a good buy point) and Trade (is the risk/reward worth it). Overall = 30% Quality + 25% Trend + 25% Entry + 20% Trade and is only used for sorting; verdicts come from the four scores. Headlines and 8-K events never change a score. Buy zone needs Entry 75+, Trend 60+, Quality 60+, Trade 60+, reward/risk of 1.5 : 1 or better, chase risk below high and no earnings inside 14 days; a strong asset that misses says what is missing. Green-tinted cells rank in the top 10% (at least top 3) of all tracked stocks on that measure, so strengths show even when the composite is lower.
